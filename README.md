One front page that sums up the world's news of Saturday, 21 November 2026: 50 dated stories from every region and field — COP31 running into overtime in Antalya, the magnitude 6.7 earthquake in Peru, the AI stock sell-off, the capture of 'El Chapo Isidro' in Mexico, the ICJ's genocide ruling on Myanmar — with a UTC timeline of the day as it unfolded.

The image is drawn as a newspaper front page (HTML/CSS rendered to PNG, no image API): a masthead naming the day, a lead story, two second stories, a UTC timeline strip, and 47 further stories in four columns. `main.ts` copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
